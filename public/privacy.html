Privacy Policy

Last updated: [[DATE]].

Phosphor is an ANSI art editor that runs in your browser, at phosphor.work. Its SOCIAL tab is optional: a lobby, rooms where you can watch someone draw, chat, and accounts. This policy says what each part collects, why, who else sees it, and how long it's kept. We apply the GDPR's standards to everyone, wherever they live.

Who we are

Phosphor is run by [[OPERATOR: your name or company, and its postal address]]. Write to us about privacy at [[privacy email]].

The editor keeps your art in your browser

Images you open, art you draw, your palettes, your fonts and your settings stay in your browser (its local storage and IndexedDB). We never receive them, unless you host a room (below). Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. The app itself is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, sees your IP address when your browser loads it.

The SOCIAL tab

Nothing from SOCIAL runs until you open its tab (or follow a link from one of our emails). From then on:

  • Your browser's key. Your browser makes a signing key and keeps it. Only its public half is sent to us, inside the short-lived pass that lets you into the lobby and rooms. It proves your messages come from you.
  • Guests have no account. You get a random nickname for the visit, and others see it while you're in the lobby or a room.
  • Accounts. When you create one, we keep your email address, your username, your password (as a one-way hash, never the password itself), when you agreed to these policies, whether you've confirmed your email, and when you last changed your username.
  • Discord and GitHub. If you sign in with them, they share your account ID, name, email and profile picture with us, and give us access tokens, which we keep with your account and use only to sign you in. Each does this as an independent company under its own privacy policy.
  • Two-factor. If you turn it on, we keep its secret and your backup codes, encrypted. "Trust this computer" leaves a cookie in your browser for 30 days.
  • Sessions. Each time you sign in, we record the IP address and browser it came from, until that session ends (when you sign out, or after 7 days).
  • Blocks. You can block people. Your list is kept until you unblock them or delete your account.
  • Cookies. We set only the cookies that keep you signed in and protect sign-in (the session, a two-factor step in progress, and a trusted computer). None of them track you.

What others see

  • In the lobby, everyone there sees your username or nickname, whether you're a guest, and the signal you choose. A public room's title, its watcher count, and a small picture of the host's art are listed for everyone. Private rooms aren't listed.
  • In a room, your browser connects directly to the host's browser. Each of you can see the other's IP address, which can show your rough location and your internet provider. If you'd rather hide yours, use a VPN. Everyone in a room sees the host's art as it's drawn.
  • Chat goes from browser to browser through the host. It never passes through our servers, and we don't store it. Whoever's in the room sees it, and any of them could copy it.

Keeping SOCIAL safe

  • Rate limits. To stop password guessing and spam, we count requests by IP address and by the email or username typed in, for about a minute.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person is creating an account or asking for a password reset.
  • Breached passwords. When you choose a password, we send the first five characters of its SHA-1 hash, never the password, to Have I Been Pwned, and refuse a password that has appeared in a breach.

Email

We email you only to confirm your address and to reset your password, through Mailjet. No newsletters and no marketing.

Why we use it (GDPR Article 6)

  • To provide SOCIAL (Art. 6(1)(b)): your account, signing in, the lobby and rooms.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): keeping SOCIAL secure, stopping abuse, and deciding on reports.
  • Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)): when the law requires us to keep or hand over something.

Who else handles it

We don't sell or share personal data, and we don't show ads. These services handle it for us, each only to run Phosphor:

  • Cloudflare: the SOCIAL server, its database and storage, rate limits and Turnstile.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the app.
  • Mailjet: sending our emails.
  • Have I Been Pwned: the breached-password check (a five-character hash prefix only).

Some of them process data outside your country. Where data leaves the EU or the UK, we rely on their data processing terms and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, until you delete it. Deleting it from the Account page removes it straight away, with your sessions, linked Discord and GitHub accounts, two-factor and blocks.
  • Sessions: until they end, at most 7 days.
  • Rate-limit counts: about a minute.
  • Reports: [[RETENTION]].
  • The lobby and rooms keep nothing after a room ends or you leave.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to have it in a portable format. Change your username, or delete your account, from the Account page. For anything else, write to [[privacy email]] from your account's email address. We answer within 30 days, free. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

SOCIAL is for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we'll delete it.
